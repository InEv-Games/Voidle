# Caretaker Barks: Type 0 Milestones (v3)

Speakers: **Mother Superior** (MS) and **High Father** (HF). Each bark fires once, on its event.
Transmuted from the parent-voiced draft at fidelity 4, within a 20–40 word bark cap. Corpus v1.

---

## Act I: Home world

### 1 · First City founded
**MS:** Our first City. Foundations sound, power steady on the line, one street already named. Well done.
**HF:** We used to tell you seeds about cities at bedtime. Now we have built one, so tonight you can tell us.

### 2a · First Energy district
**HF:** Our first Energy district. Everything else we build will lean on it, so look after it. Read its load now and then, the way we used to take your temperature.

### 2b · First Production district
**MS:** A Production district. It is loud, but it is ours, and it is working.
**HF:** Listen to it now and then. A worn bearing whines for days before it seizes. Machines tell you they are unwell long before they stop.

### 2c · First Science district
**HF:** Our first Science district. Ask it hard questions. That is what it is for, and it will not tire of them.
**MS:** You never stopped asking us why. Now you have somewhere else to ask.

### 2d · First Residential district
**MS:** Our first Residential district, and room to spare tonight.

### 2e · First Commercial district
**MS:** Our first Commercial district. In the archive, markets smelled of bread and rain. This one smells of sealant and new paint. Give it time.
**HF:** And watch the ledgers. Fairness is easier to pour with the foundations than to add later.

### 3 · First Ferrite extracted
**MS:** Ferrite. From the Latin for iron. We found it in the ground ourselves, and that matters more than the tonnage.
**HF:** The first thing this world has given us that we did not bring with us.

### 4 · First Goods produced
**HF:** Our first Consumer Goods. Rock in, use out. You have just learned the whole of industry; the rest is the same routine with more steps.

### 5 · Trade Access researched
**MS:** Trade Access. As a spicies, you leaned on this aspect of social living for many years.

### 6 · First energy shortfall
**MS:** Careful. Demand has passed supply, and production is slowing. Build power before you build anything else.
**HF:** Nothing is broken. You grew faster than your wires, the way you once grew out of your clothes. A good problem to have.

### 7 · First population shortfall
**MS:** Every room is spoken for, and the work is waiting at the door. Build housing next.

### 8 · Fuel Processing researched
**HF:** Fuel Processing. Something we make only so that it can be spent on the way up.
**MS:** Up. We have waited a long time to hear that word.

### 9 · Capital District Lv2
**MS:** Capital District, level two. It can carry heavier work now, and heavier work is coming: pads, gantries, things that leave.

### 10 · City Lv2
**HF:** Grow it on purpose.

### 11 · Planet Lv2
**HF:** Planetary level two. Load, supply and grid all agree: this world will carry another City now.
**MS:** One City is a home. Two is a map. Go and draw it.

### 12 · Second City founded
**MS:** A second City. Now we have somewhere to miss when we are in the other.

### 13 · Rare Processing researched
**HF:** Rare Processing. Patient work.
**MS:** Store it carefully.

### 14 · Rocketry researched
**HF:** Rocketry. Leaving is arithmetic: so much speed, so much fuel, so much mass you agree to leave behind.

### 15 · Spaceport built
**MS:** Spaceport complete. Pad sintered and sound, seals tested twice. Moving...
**HF:** Well done.

---

## Act II: The Moon

### 16 · Lunar Programme researched
**MS:** Lunar Programme. A lander, sized for the nearest body we can reach.
**HF:** We used to show you seedlings the Moon before bed. Now you are going to plan a trip there.

### 17 · Lander prepared
**HF:** Thirty seconds of checks: seals, tanks, guidance, seals again.

### 18 · Lander launched and landed
**HF:** Touchdown. Level, sealed, intact. The dust is already down; with no air to hold it, it simply falls. Well done.
**MS:** The first mark we have ever left anywhere but home.

### 19 · First Moon City founded
**MS:** A City on the Moon. Two worlds now, and you keep them both lit.
**HF:** Watch the habitat pressure above everything else. Out there, with vacuum on the other side of the wall, it is the one number that forgives nothing.

### 20 · First Moonstone extracted
**HF:** First Moonstone. Good work.

### 21 · Lunar Materials researched
**MS:** Regolith baked into brick, walls made from the ground they stand on.

### 22 · 100 Moonstone mined
**HF:** One hundred Moonstone as a mile stone.
**MS:** Father is always the most saucy one.

### 23 · Moon Capital Lv2
**MS:** The ground there can carry a launch pad now, and the climb out from there is shallower than from home.
**HF:** You are nearly ready. Do not rush the last steps.

### 24 · Lunar Spaceport built
**HF:** Lunar Spaceport complete. Pad cool, seals tested twice. The Moon is now the doorstep. Look how far you have come from the first street.

---

## Act III: Launch

### 25 · Advanced Planetary Rocket researched
**HF:** Getting ready to fly.
**MS:** Поехали.

### 26 · Storage expanded to 1,200
**MS:** Storage raised to twelve hundred. The rocket needs more stone than the old store could hold. Good thinking.

### 27 · Rocket preparation started
**HF:** Preparation started. Sixty seconds: tanks, frame, guidance, then tanks again. Stay with me.
**MS:** Breathe. You have done everything right so far.

### 28 · Target planet chosen
**HF:** Target set. We have the transfer orbit. If you change your mind, I will draw another.

### 29 · Launch and flight
**HF:** Ignition. Ascent nominal.
**MS:** I have the oddest feeling I have watched you do this before. From exactly this window.

### 30 · Arrival
**HF:** Arrival signal received. Ha. You did it.
**MS:** We are so proud of you.
